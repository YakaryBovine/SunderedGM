## The kid and his nukes
The camera fades out, then back in on a blurred view. The music kicks back in slowly. Our vision slowly becomes clearer, and we see it: a pattern of 81 screens. Each one shows the same thing: data pointing to the location of a compact disc in a thin metallic case. We pan backwards to see an exhausted teenager in a black hoodie slumped over his desk.

Bam! The camera cuts to a hulking giant of a woman slamming open a door. From an aerial view, we see her stride to the boy, shove him awake, and push a digital device under his face. We all look closer: it's a GPS tracker, flashing green at a point in the Shattered Expanse that overlaps precisely with one of the 81 screens. The boy smiles, the giant nods. She throws her cloak over her shoulder and heads for the door.
## Exile
Fade out. We fade in to a wide shot of a massive nuclear mushroom cloud, only it's not growing larger nor smaller, nor is there a shockwave. We pan out, slowly and just barely, to see the surrounding land: blackened ash and a putrid green glow. The mushroom cloud seems to rotate. It looks at us. A face appears, grinning and malicious.

The camera spins around 180 degrees, then speeds forward. It's like we're running away. We run from the blackened lands into a veritable heavenscape of deep greens and a luscious blue sky. But we don't stop. We see a blue portal in the distance - we rush towards it - and come out the other side. We catapult through, rush quickly from a castle structure over a small island and into the sky. We angle down - to the Sunlancer, and begin rushing towards it.
## The Sunlancer
We cut, suddenly, to the inside of a structure filled with incomprehensible golden cogs. We pan around. More cogs. We keep panning, then spin, and we're facing towards the center of the structure. We see an angel, resplendent in light, with his great wings outstretched, furled around the structure's machinery. He fills the camera view. Suddenly, the screen goes white and a horrific explosive sound drowns out the music. They both fade, slowly, until we see the outline of the angel and can just barely hear his screams as he burns. Moments later, it's over. We can see him properly.

We cut to a closeup of his face. He exhales deeply, and closes his eyes. He opens them again, urgently. Then closed again. Then his face falls from view.

The camera cuts to a downward shot of the angel falling. We wait a moment, then fall with him. The camera comes to a sudden halt at the same time the angel does, suspended in midair. We calmly rotate to a wide shot of four surprised figures; a skeleton, a tattooed warlock, a wandering collector, and a duelist.

The players are returning to Forsyning. They've been to Depot D before. Depot B and C are destroyed, so Depot A is the only remaining one.

Depot A houses a fully functional nuclear missile. Mages from Splint arrived in the last Sunlancer cycle to infiltrate it and activate the missile, but they were betrayed by the most powerful among them, Galbatoros, who sought to leave his compatriots underground and gain sole control of Splint after firing the nuclear missile. 

The ultimate goal of the mages was to use the missile to destroy the Earth Primal, as Kolthaea is burning it up to enhance her powers enough to maintain the Ashstorm.

The mages can't leave of their own accord because their bodies are weakened from Leyline Dust withdrawal, and now starvation. Galbatoros took the majority of the Leyline Dust supply, leaving each Wizard with only their personal supply. If any Wizard could get one of the other Wizard's supply, they'd be able to teleport out.
## The Wizard
There are six mages in total.

**Aureltharos:** In the entrance hallway. He's utterly screwed, malnourished, has no supply of his own and is about to die. If a player touches him, he'll teleport them both just above the chasm, and when falling, he'll grab onto the railway track. There's nothing stopping the player from doing the same.

**Galbatoros:** In the center, actively trying to launch the nuke. Incredible gravity mage.
**Valerion:** In the top left. His room is filled with 30 corpses, which turn out to be undead. He's a necromancer.
**Dravatorus:** In the top right. He's a cryomancer, and will fight with abandon.
**Malverax:** In the right. He's a demonologist, and has summoned a number of demons to tend to him.
**Octavarius:** In the bottom right. An incredible illusionist, he presents a highly charismatic front to the players, and if they try to leave he renders the door invisible. He offers to help the party reach the middle.
**Severator:** In the bottom left. He's a hemomancer, and will fight with abandon.
**Empty:** Nobody is in the left. It's just storage. However, a serious inspection of the room reveals launch codes, and a way to stop the nuke firing if you can get to the central computer.
## Layout
The layout of the depot is six hexagonal storage units suspended above a deep void, connected only by railway tracks that go around the outside. The railway car can only be accessed via keycard.

## Entrance
The entrance is a decoy. It's a small room with a counter and a locked back storage filled with basic Stalrad supplies. There's a camera embedded in one wall that registers Stalrad visitors and allows them entrance.

However, the wall has already been exploded and replaced by an illusion. The players can just walk through it.
## The challenge
The party is introduced to the challenge by Aureltharos. If the party wants the ultimate treasures of the Depot, they'll have to reach the nuclear silo in the center. However, none of the mages from Splint could figure out how to get in physically. Galbatoros took their powder supply and teleported in alone.

All of the mages have enough power to teleport themselves inside the central silo, but alone none of them can escape to the surface. Most of the mages will help the player reach the center, but only if they can get powder by killing another mage. None of them will ever trust each other. So, the players have to decide who to help and who to hurt. Alternatively, if they put serious effort in, they could figure out the mechanism to reach the center: the railway can be directed. However, attempting this too early will be prevented by Galbatoros' gravity magic.

## Boss fight!
In the center, Galbatoros is trying to launch the nuclear missile. This would be catastrophic for everyone. The party doesn't know what kind of progress he's making, but if he gets the codes from them he'll be sorted.
### Phase 0: Deal with the other mages
If the player arrived without dealing with every single Mage, they'll first have to fight and kill any of the ones that survived, as they all teleport in to do battle.
### Phase 1: Gravity
Galbatoros, upon seeing the players, will engage his gravity magic, turning the direction of gravity directly outwards, such that all of the walls become the floor. He will also rip metal from the room and form 2 concentric circles of metal walls around himself, each with irregular gaps, such that there is no visibility from the outside of the room to the center.

Each round, Galbatoros will rotate his walls predictably, then emphasize his gravity magic and violently push everyone back to the ground, dealing fall damage.
### Phase 2: Floating rocks
The first time Galbatoros is reduced to 0 HP, he will let loose a gravity blast, pushing his own rings out to the walls along with the players, inflicting fall damage (half on a successful Evasion). He'll take a huge snort of his powder, raise a number of floating rocks around the room. To reach the center again, players will have to jump to these floating rocks, and to avoid damage, will have to stay underneath them.
### Phase 3: No walls
The second time Galbatoros is reduced to 0 HP, he lets loose a gravity blast so powerful it shatters the walls of the central chamber entirely, revealing the entire area and depleting his energies. Here, the players have no choice: they must engage the train to reach the center.
